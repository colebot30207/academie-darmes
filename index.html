About the Academy

Académie d'Armes is proud to carry forward the rich legacy of the distinguished Lambertville fencing academy that has served the Bucks, Montgomery, Mercer, and Hunterdon county communities for over 45 years. Honoring this tradition of excellence, Académie d'Armes is committed to inspiring the next generation of fencers through exceptional instruction, competitive development, and a continued passion for the sport.
